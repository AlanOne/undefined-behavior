Omarchy Notes is a small, honest collection of guides for running Omarchy, Hyprland,
and a self-hosted Linux setup day to day. No sponsored fluff, no AI-generated listicles —
just what actually worked when setting this stuff up on a real machine.

Some links on this site are affiliate/referral links to tools and services mentioned in the
guides. If you use one, it costs you nothing extra and helps keep this site running. Every
recommendation here is used personally first — nothing gets linked just because it pays.
